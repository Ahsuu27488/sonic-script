        Speak Urdu, Hindi or English, mixed as you like: it types Roman Urdu / Hinglish where your cursor is. Open source (MIT).

        Privacy

        • No SonicScript server: your voice goes from this PC straight to the provider of your key (Google, Groq, or OpenAI).
        • On Google's free tier, Google may use requests to improve its products and people may review them; the paid tier does not.
        • History and your words stay on this PC. Keys stay in Windows Credential Manager.
