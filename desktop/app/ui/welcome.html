Bolo, hum likh denge.

Speak Urdu, Hindi or English, mixed as you like. SonicScript types it in Roman letters wherever your cursor is. Two steps and you are ready.

1

Add your free Gemini key

Google gives it free. Sign in, press Create API key, copy it, paste it here.

Groq key Optional, makes it better

With it, SonicScript uses its most accurate model first. Free at Groq.

2

Tap Right Ctrl and speak

Click in the box, tap the key, say something, then tap it again (or press Enter). Or hold the key while you speak.

SonicScript lives in the tray (by the clock). Change the key, models and words in its Settings.
